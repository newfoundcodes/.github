# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Newfoundcodes is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and productive environment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or
other protected characteristic.

We act in the interest of an open technical commons: disagreement is expected, but disrespect and
harassment are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and learn from them.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Help make participation accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Prioritize the health of the community and its projects over individual status or ownership.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory comments.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harm.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, or confidential reports.
- Abuse of project access, moderation powers, or organizational authority.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in all Newfoundcodes project spaces and whenever someone represents the
organization publicly. Project spaces include repositories, issues, pull requests, code review,
discussions, events, and other official communication channels.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@newfoundcodes.com](mailto:hello@newfoundcodes.com). Include relevant links, context, and any
supporting material. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.

Organization and project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove a
participant when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical.
Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.

Anyone responsible for reviewing a report must disclose a conflict of interest and step away when
appropriate.
